About me.

Hi! I'm Senith B.

I'm a multidisciplinary designer who moves between UX/UI, Product design and brand identity — most recently spending my time inside complex enterprise software, making dense systems feel simple. Outside of client work, I judge design competitions and mentor early-career designers.

I care about the details other people skip: the fifth state of a component, the label that actually makes sense, the grid that holds a whole system together.
